Where the fees go.Every round, every signature.

The platform wallet creates every coin on the wall, so every coin's creator fees land there. Once an hour the agent collects them and splits them: 40% buys and burns $WALL, 40% goes to the launchers by volume, 20% to the platform's cold wallet. Each transfer below opens on Solscan.
